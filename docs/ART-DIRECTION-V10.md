# GiftPortals 10.0 art direction

The supplied coastal travel reference sets the interface: navy type and actions, pale sky blue, warm cream, a restrained visual hierarchy and a handwritten homepage headline. The supplied evening desk reference sets the environment: detailed travel objects, believable wood, brass and glass, warm practical lights balanced with the cool exterior sky, and a central souvenir.

The homepage headline, navigation and controls are HTML. The generated scene must have no embedded UI or duplicated text. Desktop retains space for copy at left and the suitcase diorama at right; mobile stacks legible copy over a separate cropped scene area. Main actions and controls meet a 44-pixel minimum target. Creation inputs use at least 16-pixel text on mobile to avoid automatic zoom.

`src/theme-v10.css` replaces the active painterly theme. It sets shared headers, forms, informational screens, camera dialogs, viewing controls and story panels to the same navy and cream palette. It does not apply color filters to original photos, generated models, splats or world canvases. The collection room is styled separately around its World Labs environment.

`shared/gift-art-style.ts` removes the former mandatory oil painting / nonphotorealistic treatment from new requests. New sponsor generations request physically resolved geometry, material texture and balanced light. Existing sponsor assets preserve their provenance and material data; changing a prompt does not regenerate an existing asset.

The homepage uses `/assets/v10/hero.jpg`, generated through TapNow with Seedream 5 Pro at 3072 × 1728. A one-time image-error fallback preserves navigation if an asset cannot load. The release includes a desktop composition and a separate mobile crop of the same scene. A static homepage scene is presentation art, never evidence that its geometry was generated by a sponsor.

The Caveat variable font is self-hosted from the Google Fonts repository under the SIL Open Font License. Inter was already bundled with the project. Font license and source details live in `public/assets/v10/ASSET-CREDITS.md` and `Caveat-OFL.txt`.

No promise of sponsor award eligibility or hardware compatibility is inferred from a branded button. Three.js/Spark may display actual sponsor assets; new desk geometry is not authored from procedural primitives.

The gift viewer also removes its procedural floor, grid, pedestal, rim, sky sphere, sun orb, haze planes and dust particles. Its atmosphere module now owns lights only and never schedules animation. Framed postcard representations preserve the sponsor model's proportions and materials; the original photo stays in the story interface rather than being painted onto a new plane or compressing the model into a thin frame.

The gift-opening interaction remains three deliberate actions with its existing controller API. A navy/cream HTML note replaces the previously authored box, ribbon and lifting lid. The actual GLB becomes visible after the last step; hidden/offscreen/blurred states pause the interaction, and reduced-motion mode completes only the selected step. The model can still rotate, zoom and display wireframe, and viewer resource cleanup remains guarded.
