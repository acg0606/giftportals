Validated September 30, 2026, from the preserved `v3` baseline on branch `v4-nexus`. All product copy and materials are in English. This increment adds a complete Memory Trail rather than replacing the existing provider assets or geographic integration.

## Implemented experience

An authorized gift signal leads to its object, place and personal story. Three explicit reveals unlock an assembled postcard. Keeping it creates a session-only digital trace. The memory train returns to the same person's atlas, where the journal offers a next authorized story. A reload or change of person/account resets the trail.

The original fictional bird reference stays visible beside the actual Tripo 3D interpretation. The place uses the existing completed World Labs artistic environment. A failed viewer preserves the complete original image and story, with Retry. The optional geographic globe and God's Eye source attribution remain available.

## Scope and limits

The demo people, story and source reference are fictional. The source is a Tripo-generated illustration, not a physical souvenir photograph. The attached GLB/SPZ are actual precomputed outputs. Reveal does not generate them live. The World Labs scene is visibly soft artistic scenery, not a factual reconstruction.

Session progress stays in RAM. Its authorized semantic snapshots may include story/location values for reconciliation; they are not stored or logged, and signed URLs, expiry and gift tokens are excluded. Progress never grants access, creates a cloud claim or records a physical visit. The TUCA anchor supplies published context, not proof of where a souvenir was found.

Large existing lazy chunks remain: approximately 608 kB Three.js, 4.03 MB geographic globe and 4.93 MB environment module before gzip. Cleanup checks do not establish total GPU/RAM use, frame rate, all-device performance, WCAG conformance or screen-reader certification.

Cloud ACL/session/recipient integration, production deployment and hackathon submission remain pending configuration/access. No NEXUS walkthrough video was recorded in this increment; the English 115-second outline is a script, not a recording receipt. The local preview, tests and screenshots do not establish those external outcomes.
