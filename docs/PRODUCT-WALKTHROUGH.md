# GiftPortals — product walkthrough

The current English narrated video presents the product through Paris: a real photograph and personal words, creator review, a completed Tripo keepsake, a brief World Labs visit, the memory newspaper and optional preparation for a physical gift. It focuses on the recipient experience and provider contributions.

[Watch the product walkthrough](https://giftportals.vercel.app/media/giftportals-v11.1-narrated-walkthrough.mp4). Duration: 106 seconds. [Previous release walkthrough](https://giftportals.vercel.app/media/giftportals-v11.1-release-walkthrough.mp4).

## Narration

What if a photograph could become a place you could give to someone?

GiftPortals turns a real photo, a story, and a dedication into a personal gift: a three-dimensional souvenir that opens into an immersive world.

Start with a place you love. Choose its photograph, add your memories, and tell us who the gift is for. Review, then create your little world.

Tripo creates the textured souvenir. Turn it around, move closer, and enjoy the details. Here's a completed gift inspired by Paris: a little piece of a place, made to be discovered.

Then your souvenir becomes a doorway. With World Labs, GiftPortals opens an immersive interpretation of the photograph. Someone who has never visited Paris can explore its atmosphere and imagine being there.

The original photo, story, and dedication stay together in the memory newspaper. Every gift creates a connection with a place, a memory, and the person who shared it.

For a physical gift, export the souvenir for review in HeyGears Blueprint and a possible 3D print.

Built with TypeScript and Vite, Three.js, Spark and Rapier, with Supabase storage and Vercel hosting. TapNow and OpenAI supported the artwork, visual direction and development.

Created for Tripothon, organized by Tripo. Thank you to World Labs, TapNow, HeyGears, PICO, Jupiter SR, and all the event's sponsors and partners.

GiftPortals. From a photograph, to a souvenir, to a world—and potentially, a gift you can hold.

## Source and claim boundaries

The Paris photograph is “Overview Seine River to Eiffel Tower” by Alex Liivet, [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), [original source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Overview_Seine_River_to_Eiffel_Tower_(37578867564).jpg). Its World Labs world uses that photographic reference and infers unseen areas. The demonstrated Paris Tripo miniature is an earlier completed asset with a separate source history. The creator flow and completed Paris example are not presented as an instantaneous generation or a claim that this exact miniature came from the newer source photograph.

GiftPortals exports the original GLB, scaled STL and ZIP locally. These are files for review, not a certified print-ready result. HeyGears documents [STL/GLB file formats](https://store.heygears.com/blogs/blog/3d-print-file-formats) and [Blueprint preparation, slicing and printer workflow](https://wiki.heygears.com/en/Reflex/materials_application/PAP10Black). Blueprint import, supports, repair, slicing and physical printing have not been validated for this keepsake. The narration describes a possible physical gift.

Tripo and World Labs are the core creation providers; TypeScript/Vite, Three.js/Spark, Rapier, Supabase and Vercel provide the application, rendering, movement, storage and hosting. TapNow and OpenAI supported artwork, visual direction and development. The event-partner card identifies Tripo, World Labs, HeyGears, TapNow, PICO, Jupiter SR, Monolith, VitalBridge and ZhenFund, following the [official Tripothon page](https://developers.tripo3d.ai/en/events/tripothon-s1).
