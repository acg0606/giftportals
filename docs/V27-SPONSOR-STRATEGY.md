# GiftPortals V27 sponsor strategy

Official-source research and local evidence reviewed on 2026-10-04. The final local build passed 705 tests, client/server TypeScript and cloud preflight. No new paid generation, deployment, submission or physical hardware session occurred in this work.

## Choose evidence before breadth

**App** fits GiftPortals' current complete journey. Tripothon requires one Direction Track and permits **0–3 Tool Tracks**; a named-tool track requires actual use. Tripo and World Labs are the strongest evidenced choices. Keep a third track conditional on real tool/device evidence and organizer eligibility; two is valid. Prize pools differ between Global Online and regional offline participation, so confirm the applicable pool rather than treating a regional award as global. [Official event rules and prize pools](https://developers.tripo3d.ai/en/events/tripothon-s1).

The current presentation is a warm oil-painted memory experience: create a gift, unwrap its actual model, explore its imagined place, read its story and revisit it on a cozy Memory desk. There are **three complete asset-backed gifts and ten inspirations**. The desk/projector, wrapping, navigation, stories, atmosphere and diagnostics are original GiftPortals work; they must keep their attribution. Existing model textures and SPZ worlds remain cached originals. Future painted prompts are a recipe change, not evidence of fresh provider output.

## Prioritized next evidence

1. **Tripo + World Labs:** record one complete gift journey: approved input → actual GLB → untie/lift/reveal → walk a verified world path → find the keepsake/read its story → revisit it on the desk. Include touch/keyboard behavior, actual model/world loading and clear original/derived input labels. This demonstrates sponsor synergy with existing assets and no new generation cost.
2. **Choose a third track from real access:** if Blueprint is available, import the exact audited STL and record the reviewed sliced result; if PICO hardware is available, record the real opt-in XR session. Use one only after its proof and eligibility exist. Keep all hardware tests labeled pending until then. HeyGears is the present print-preparation candidate, not a confirmed third-track claim.
3. **Later controlled generation:** multi-image/panorama Marble creation and supported Tripo multiview can improve a new memory after approved inputs, adapted request contracts and explicit cost limits. Pin documented models, audit the returned collider and preserve generation attribution. Haze, particles and motion produced by GiftPortals do not prove an interactive generated world. See [World Labs research](V27-WORLD-LABS-RESEARCH.md) and [Tripo research](V27-TRIPO-RESEARCH.md).

This ranking is an inference from current evidence. Favor the complete emotional journey and demonstrate the strongest tools instead of selecting five sponsor tracks or guessing unsupported APIs.

## Print and delivery limits

The offline audit decoded the actual three source GLBs, retaining positions, indices and hierarchy. At 80 mm/yaw 0, exports stayed within the 25 MiB/200k-triangle limits. Rio has 29,137 triangles; Paris V17 29,186; Antikythera 27,241. All have diagnostic open/nonmanifold edges and need review. Diagnostic welding at 0.00001 mm does not establish wall thickness, self-intersections, supports, device fit or print success. STL does not preserve color textures. Exact source/STL hashes, dimensions and edge counts are in [the physical audit](V27-PHYSICAL-ASSET-AUDIT.json).

The automated IAB ZIP download timed out and actual completion remains unconfirmed despite passing package/mesh tests. One prior Spark 2.1.0 promise rejection during HMR/world teardown remains an observation, not a claimed vendor fix. See [delivery evidence and boundaries](V27-DELIVERY.md).

Before submission, verify every jury-facing link anonymously, confirm public access settings and use actual product screenshots with English captions. Preserve the existing receipt and private projects. Do not invent Jupiter endpoints, device quilts/calibration, printer controls, headset success or live cloud operation from compatibility alone.
