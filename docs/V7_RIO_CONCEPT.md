# V7 — a souvenir becomes a doorway

The approved example connects an attractive physical souvenir with the place it represents: a visitor buys a Sugarloaf Mountain miniature in Rio de Janeiro and later opens an artistic Rio memory on a phone. The object, place and message form one encounter.

## Local journey

1. Open the Rio gift. Recognize the miniature, Guanabara Bay and the cable car.
2. Select **Enter Rio**. Explore an illustrated panoramic environment inspired by the same place.
3. Read or hide the sender's message, look around, then return to the souvenir.

The visitor's purchase is the story premise. This preview has no checkout, retailer integration, scan-code binding or evidence of a physical purchase. It does not create private memories, provider jobs, gifts or recorded visits.

Asset prompt set:

- **Souvenir:** isolated tactile Sugarloaf Mountain souvenir matching the approved reference, with cable car, Guanabara Bay, small sailboats and coastal buildings, circular wooden base, a Rio de Janeiro plaque, warm ivory background, complete uncropped object and photographic material richness with fine ink contours. No phone, people, QR, tag, captions or watermark.
- **Place:** a 2:1 artistic waterfront panorama inspired by Guanabara Bay, with one central Sugarloaf Mountain and a cable car, green hills, warm late-afternoon sky, teal water, stone promenade and the approved visual palette. No text, UI, phone, hologram or nearby people. Generated continuity is not certified; viewing angles are bounded.
